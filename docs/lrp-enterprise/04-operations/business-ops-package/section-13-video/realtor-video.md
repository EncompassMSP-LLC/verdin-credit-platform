# Realtor Video

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field    | Value                             |
| -------- | --------------------------------- |
| Length   | 75–100 seconds                    |
| Audience | Real estate agents / team leaders |
| Embed    | `/realtors`, realtor kit drops    |

---

## Script

**[Hook]**

> When financing needs more time, do you lose the buyer—or keep them engaged with dignity?

**[Problem]**

> “Not yet” can feel like a dead end. Agents need language they can defend—and a path that respects the preferred lender.

**[Solution]**

> Lending Readiness Partners works alongside your buyer’s loan officer. We help organize a readiness plan—education and next steps—while you stay in the relationship.

**[What you can say]**

> “Your LO can involve a readiness partner so ‘not yet’ still has a clear plan.”

**[What you don’t say]**

> Don’t promise approval, timelines to funding, or score outcomes.

**On-screen:** Say / Don’t say cards

**[Close]**

> Keep buyers engaged when financing needs more time.  
> Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready.  
> Open the realtor kit or contact us for a briefing.

**End card:** `/realtors` · disclaimer
