# Success Story Video (Composite)

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field  | Value                                                                          |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Length | 90–120 seconds                                                                 |
| Rule   | **Composite vignette** unless signed release on file                           |
| Label  | On-screen: “Composite vignette for illustration—not a specific client result.” |

Aligns with Section 12 [`success-stories.md`](../section-12-website/success-stories.md).

---

## Objective

Show process outcomes—plan clarity, partner visibility, return to LO—without score theater or approval claims.

---

## Script (composite CS-01 angle)

**On-screen label first 3 seconds**

**[VO]**

> A loan officer had a motivated borrower who wasn’t ready yet. The old playbook was “call me later.”

> Instead, the LO referred to Lending Readiness Partners. The borrower got a clear plan—education, documents, paced tasks. The LO received updates they could explain.

> No one promised a score. No one promised approval. When the timing was right, the borrower returned for the next financing conversation—better prepared.

> That’s the partnership: Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready.

**End card:** Disclaimer · composite label · CTA (book briefing)
