# Whiteboard Series

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field   | Value                                              |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Format  | Presenter + whiteboard / lightboard · 4–7 min each |
| Cadence | Monthly after launch set                           |
| Tone    | Teaching, calm, precise                            |

---

## Episode template

1. Hook question (10 sec)
2. Board title
3. 3–5 teaching points
4. Boundary reminder
5. CTA + disclaimer

---

## Launch set (6 episodes)

### WB-01 — What “lending ready” means

**Board:** Ready ≠ Approved  
**Points:** Habits · Documents · Time · Partner visibility  
**Close:** Advisory readiness prepares the conversation; lenders decide.

### WB-02 — Refer → Plan → Update → Return

**Board:** Four boxes with arrows  
**Points:** Who owns each step · What “update” includes · When return happens  
**Close:** No unsupervised filing theater.

### WB-03 — What LOs can say (and can’t)

**Board:** Two columns: Say / Don’t say  
**Say:** “Not yet can still have a plan.”  
**Don’t:** “We’ll get you approved / to 740.”  
**Close:** Use partner-kit language only.

### WB-04 — What realtors can say

**Board:** Buyer conversation map  
**Points:** Dignity · Preferred lender stays · Time ≠ failure  
**Close:** Link to `/realtors` scripts.

### WB-05 — Advisory score vs bureau score

**Board:** Two circles — no overlap labeled “approval”  
**Points:** Lending Readiness Score™ organizes work · Not FICO · Not underwriting  
**Disclaimer:** On-screen full short disclaimer

### WB-06 — Documents before the conversation

**Board:** Checklist clusters (ID, income, housing, credit topics at high level)  
**Points:** Portal tasks · No SSN on camera · Partner updates  
**Close:** Ask LO about readiness support.
