# Welcome / Brand Video

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field  | Value                             |
| ------ | --------------------------------- |
| Length | 60–90 seconds                     |
| Aspect | 16:9 master · 9:16 cutdown        |
| Use    | Home page, about, partner kit hub |

---

## Objective

Introduce the brand and posture in one composition: who we are, who we serve, what we never claim.

---

## Script

**[0:00–0:08] Cold open (VO + brand)**

> When the answer is “not yet,” the relationship shouldn’t end.

**On-screen:** Lending Readiness Partners

**[0:08–0:25] Who we are**

> We’re Lending Readiness Partners. We help more borrowers become lending ready—with education, a clear plan, and progress partners can see.

**[0:25–0:45] Boundary**

> We’re not a lender. We don’t approve loans. We don’t promise scores or funding. Your loan officer underwrites. We help prepare for that conversation.

**On-screen disclaimer**

**[0:45–0:70] Who it’s for**

> Built for loan officers, realtors, and referral partners who want dignity-first support when financing needs more time.

**[0:70–0:90] Close**

> Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready.  
> Explore the partner kit—or ask your loan officer about Lending Readiness Partners.

**End card:** Wordmark · tagline · URL · short disclaimer

---

## B-roll notes

Calm homeownership atmosphere, desk conversation, tablet portal UI (no PII), handshake optional—avoid “destroyed credit” clichés.
