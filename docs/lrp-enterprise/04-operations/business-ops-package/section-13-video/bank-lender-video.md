# Bank / Lender Video

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field    | Value                                                 |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Length   | 90–150 seconds                                        |
| Audience | Banks, credit unions, brokerages, production managers |
| Use      | Executive briefings, LO huddles, `/lenders`           |

---

## Script

**[Open]**

> Production teams don’t need another lead vendor. They need a claim-safe way to keep near-miss borrowers in the conversation.

**[Positioning]**

> Lending Readiness Partners is a Mortgage Readiness Partnership—advisory plans, partner visibility, and staff-mediated process where required.

**[Ops fit]**

> Referrals enter a clear loop: Refer, Plan, Update, Return. Your underwriters still underwrite. Your LOs still own the financing conversation.

**[Risk controls]**

> No guaranteed approval marketing. No fabricated scores. Dispute work is supervised. Progress reporting is built for partners—not for confusing borrowers about credit decisions.

**On-screen disclaimer**

**[Proof of enablement — not outcomes]**

> Partners receive training, claim-safe kits, and status they can explain. Borrowers receive education and a paced plan.

**[Close]**

> Help more borrowers become lending ready—without underwriting confusion.  
> Schedule a partnership briefing today.

**End card:** `/lenders` · tagline · disclaimer
