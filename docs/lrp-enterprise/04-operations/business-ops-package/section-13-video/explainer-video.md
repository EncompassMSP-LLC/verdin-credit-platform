## Script (VO)

**Chapter 1**

> Getting to a mortgage conversation takes more than hope. Credit gaps, documentation, and timing can pause even motivated borrowers.

**Chapter 2**

> Lending Readiness Partners helps organize that work. We are not a credit bureau, not a lender, and not a guarantee factory.

**Chapter 3**

> Partners refer. We build a plan. We update with authorized progress. The borrower returns for the next financing conversation.

**Chapter 4**

> Borrowers get education, tasks, and a portal—with dignity. No shame language. No miracle timelines.

**Chapter 5**

> Loan officers and realtors get visibility without underwriting confusion—status they can explain.

**Chapter 6**

> Disputes, when appropriate, are staff-reviewed. Marketing stays claim-safe. The Lending Readiness Score™ is advisory only.

**On-screen disclaimer**

**Chapter 7**

> Ask your loan officer—or partners, book a briefing and open the digital kit.  
> Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready.
