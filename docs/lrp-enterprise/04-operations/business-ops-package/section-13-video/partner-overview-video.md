# Partner Overview Video

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field    | Value                                    |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Length   | 90–120 seconds                           |
| Audience | Lenders, brokers, branch managers        |
| Embed    | `/lenders`, partner kit, sales follow-up |

---

## Objective

Explain the Mortgage Readiness Partnership model and Refer → Plan → Update → Return loop.

---

## Script

**[Open]**

> Loan officers: when a borrower isn’t ready yet, what happens to that relationship?

**[Problem]**

> Too often, “call me later” becomes silence. Motivated borrowers stall. Production teams lose visibility.

**[Solution]**

> Lending Readiness Partners is your advisory readiness team. We organize education, documentation habits, and staff-mediated next steps where appropriate—while you stay informed.

**[Process — on-screen steps]**

1. Refer
2. Plan
3. Update
4. Return

> The borrower returns to you for the financing conversation—clearer, not promised.

**[Benefits — 4 beats]**

- Named partnership contact
- Partner-visible progress
- Claim-safe marketing you can defend
- Flexible engagement—no exclusivity theater

**[Boundary]**

> We support readiness. Your team underwrites. Lending Readiness Score™ is advisory—not an approval.

**On-screen disclaimer**

**[Close]**

> Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready.  
> Book a briefing or open the partner kit today.
