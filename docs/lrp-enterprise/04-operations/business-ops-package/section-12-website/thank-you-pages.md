# Thank-You Pages

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

Post-submit confirmation pages. No new promises. Always include next step + short disclaimer.

---

## Routes

| Path                  | Trigger                                | Message focus                                     |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `/thank-you/lender`   | Lender briefing / kit request          | Packet en route; calendar / booking link          |
| `/thank-you/realtor`  | Realtor kit / contact                  | Kit + preferred-lender note                       |
| `/thank-you/borrower` | Borrower contact                       | LO will follow up; portal invite only if enrolled |
| `/thank-you/referral` | Referral form submitted                | Received; acknowledgment SLA                      |
| `/thank-you/advisor`  | Attorney / planner / builder / advisor | Partner Success will respond                      |
| `/thank-you/contact`  | Generic contact                        | We received your message                          |

---

## Page composition

1. Brand wordmark
2. One thank-you headline
3. One short next-step sentence
4. Optional secondary link (kit, FAQ, login)
5. Short advisory disclaimer

**Example (lender):**

> Thanks — your partnership request is in.  
> A Partner Success teammate will follow up with your briefing materials.  
> Meanwhile, open the digital partner kit.

---

## SLA copy (ops, not public promises of outcomes)

| Audience                   | Internal ack target                                      |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |

| Referral form              | Confirm receipt ≤ 1 business day to referring partner    |
| Borrower                   | Route to LO / case owner; do not imply approval timeline |
