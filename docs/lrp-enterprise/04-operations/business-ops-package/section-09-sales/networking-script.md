# Networking Script

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

---

## Mixer / association (30–45 sec)

> I’m with **Lending Readiness Partners**—we help borrowers prepare for the next financing conversation and keep loan officers informed. We’re not a lender and we don’t guarantee approvals. If you ever have clients who need a plan instead of “call me later,” I’d love to share our partner kit.

---

## Chamber intro (30–45 sec)

> Good morning—I’m [Name] with Lending Readiness Partners. We provide mortgage readiness support for lenders, realtors, and borrowers: education, structured plans, and partner visibility. We don’t underwrite or guarantee approvals. If your members work with homebuyers who need time, we’re a claim-safe partner. Happy to connect afterward.

---

## Trade show greeter

**Ask:** “Are you originating or partnering with loan officers?”  
**If yes:** “Here’s our LO quick reference—scan for the digital kit. Want a two-minute walkthrough of the referral loop?”  
**Hand:** Rack card + QR · badge scan for follow-up

---

## Elevator (15 sec)
