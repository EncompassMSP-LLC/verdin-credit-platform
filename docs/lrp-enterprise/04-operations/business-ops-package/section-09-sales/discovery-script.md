# Discovery Script (15–20 min)

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

---

## Agenda

| Minute | Goal                                               |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–2    | Rapport + confirm role                             |
| 2–5    | Current near-miss handling                         |
| 5–8    | Pain: fallout, status chaos, compliance fear       |
| 8–12   | Partnership model (Refer → Plan → Update → Return) |
| 12–15  | Boundaries: advisory only; staff-mediated          |
| 15–18  | Kit leave-behind + pilot suggestion                |
| 18–20  | Next step: kickoff or send packet                  |

---

## Opening

> Thanks for the time. I’ll keep this tight: understand how you handle “not yet” borrowers, share how Lending Readiness Partners works, and see if a small pilot makes sense. We’re advisory—not a lender—and we don’t guarantee approvals.

---

## Discovery questions

1. Roughly how many files per month stall on credit or docs?
2. Who owns borrower communication when it’s “not yet”?
3. What would “good updates” look like for your LOs?
4. Any compliance constraints on co-marketing or referral partners?
5. Ideal pilot size for 90 days?
6. Who else should see the Partner Welcome Guide?

---

## Teach-back (after answers)

> What I’m hearing is [pain]. Our model: you refer in a few minutes, we run a readiness plan with the borrower, and—if they authorize it—you see workflow status until a mortgage-ready hand-back. Approval and underwriting stay with you.

Show: LO Quick Start · pipeline stages (ops) · sample weekly report (redacted).

---

## Boundaries (must say)

- Advisory Lending Readiness Score™ ≠ FICO / approval
- Staff review before dispute sending
- Non-exclusive; no required-use of LRP for loan terms

---

## Pilot ask

> Let’s start with [5–10] referrals over [30–60] days, LO Quick Start for your team, and a 30-day check-in. If it’s not useful, we part as friends.

---

## Close

> Next step: I’ll send the onboarding kit and propose two kickoff times. Sound good?
