# Text Message Templates

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

**TCPA:** Only text contacts with prior consent. Identify LRP. Offer STOP. Prefer portal/email for sensitive detail.

---

## B2B — LO (consented)

**Intro:**

> Hi [Name], [You] with Lending Readiness Partners. OK to text about a 15-min readiness partnership briefing? Reply YES or STOP.

**After YES:**

> Thanks—Tue 10am or Thu 2pm work? I’ll email the LO Quick Start too. We’re advisory only (not a lender).

**Reminder:**

> Reminder: LRP briefing [Day] [Time]. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule.

---

## B2B — After missed call

> [Name]—[You] @ Lending Readiness Partners tried you about the partner kit. Prefer a call or email? Reply CALL / EMAIL / STOP.

---

## Borrower (only with consent + engagement)

**Consult reminder:**

> Hi [Name], Lending Readiness Partners reminding you of your consult on [Day] at [Time]. Reply C to confirm. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Doc nudge:**

> Hi [Name], friendly nudge from LRP—secure portal still needs [item]. Questions? Reply HELP. STOP to opt out.

**Never by SMS:** SSN, full account numbers, credit report images, score promises.
