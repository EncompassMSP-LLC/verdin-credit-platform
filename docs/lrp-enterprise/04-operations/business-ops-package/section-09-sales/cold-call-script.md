# Cold Call Script

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Audience | Loan officers, branch managers, broker-owners |
| Goal | Book a 15-minute briefing or send the partner kit |
| Rule | Full brand name first; no approval/score guarantees |

---

## Opener (permission)

> Hi [Name], this is [You] with **Lending Readiness Partners**. We help loan officers keep near-miss borrowers in the conversation with a structured mortgage readiness plan—and partner visibility. Do you have two minutes?

**If no:** “Totally fair—when’s a better 90-second window this week?”  
**If gatekeeper:** “I’m following up on mortgage readiness partnership materials for [Name]—happy to email a one-pager.”

---

## Pivot question

> When a borrower isn’t ready yet—credit, docs, or timeline—what happens to that relationship today?

Listen. Reflect one pain (fallout, “call me later,” LO coaching time).

---

## Value (30 seconds)

> We partner with lenders and LOs as an **advisory readiness** team: education, a clear plan, staff-reviewed dispute work where appropriate, and status updates when the borrower authorizes them. We’re **not** a lender and we **don’t** guarantee approval or score outcomes. The goal is a better-prepared borrower for _your_ financing conversation.

---

## Offer

> I’d like to send our LO Quick Start and book a 15-minute briefing—claim-safe materials your compliance team can review. No exclusivity required.

---

## Close (choice)

> Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready. What’s better—Tuesday or Thursday morning?

---

## Objections (short)

| Objection                                | Response                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| “We already do credit repair referrals.” | “Understood—many partners want claim-safe language and LO visibility. Happy to show the difference in 15 minutes.” |
| “Is this exclusive?”                     | “Non-exclusive by design.”                                                                                         |
| “Will you get them approved?”            | “No—we prepare borrowers for a lender conversation. Approval stays with you.”                                      |
| “Send an email.”                         | “Will do—subject line will be Lending Readiness Partners / LO Quick Start. May I confirm your email?”              |
