# Voicemail Script

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

Keep under 25 seconds. Smile. Say full company name twice.

---

## A — Cold / first touch

> Hi [Name], [You] with **Lending Readiness Partners**. We partner with loan officers on mortgage readiness—advisory plans when the answer is “not yet,” with progress updates for your team. I’ll email a leave-behind. My number is [phone]. Again, Lending Readiness Partners.

---

## B — Follow-up after email

> Hi [Name], [You] with Lending Readiness Partners following up on the LO Quick Start I emailed. Happy to do a 15-minute briefing—no exclusivity, no approval guarantees. [Phone]. Thanks.

---

## C — Post-meeting

> Hi [Name], [You] with Lending Readiness Partners. Thanks for the conversation—kicking off materials are in your inbox. Reply with a good time for LO training, or call me at [phone].

---

## D — Realtor / affiliate

> Hi [Name], [You] with Lending Readiness Partners. We help buyers get organized for a lender conversation—education and a clear plan, not loan approval. I’ll text/email a short overview. [Phone].
