**Lending Readiness Partners**

Use for the first live partner briefing after discovery (20–30 min).

## Pre-call

- [ ] Agreement draft status known (counsel version)
- [ ] Portal demo or screenshots ready

## Flow

### 1. Recap (2 min)

> You asked for a clearer path for near-miss borrowers and LO visibility. Today: how referrals work, what we never promise, and how we launch.

### 2. Demo walkthrough (8–10 min)

1. Submit referral fields
2. Acknowledgment SLA (2 business days)
3. Stages (workflow, not underwriting)
4. Authorized status updates
5. Mortgage-ready hand-back

### 3. Enablement (5 min)

- LO Quick Start
- Office Training Manual (RESPA-aware)
- Claim-library language card

### 4. Commercial / ops (3 min)

- Fee model as approved by finance/counsel
- Primary contacts + escalation
- Reporting cadence

### 5. Decisions (5 min)

| Decision           | Options |
| ------------------ | ------- |
| Pilot vs full      |         |
