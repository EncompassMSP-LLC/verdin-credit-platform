# Executive Sales Deck (30–40 slides)

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field    | Value                                                                 |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Audience | Banks, mortgage companies, credit unions, community banks, brokerages |
| Length   | 30–40 slides                                                          |
| Tone     | Credible financial-professional; not aggressive CRO marketing         |

---

## Slide map

### 1–4 · Open

1. **Title** — Lending Readiness Partners · tagline
2. **Agenda** — Problem · Partnership · Process · Proof points · Next step
3. **Disclaimer** — Advisory readiness; not a lender; no guaranteed approval/funding
4. **The missed opportunity** — Borrowers who want to proceed but aren’t documentation/credit-ready

### 5–10 · Problem

5. Fallout from premature applications
6. LO time spent as informal credit coach
7. Inconsistent borrower expectations
8. Pipeline stalls without a partner process
9. Compliance risk from over-promising language
10. What “lending ready” means here (definition slide)

### 11–18 · Solution

11. Who we are / brand posture
12. What we do / don’t do
13. Referral partnership model (non-exclusive)
14. Roles: Partner · LO · LRP · Borrower
15. Lending Readiness Score™ (advisory callout + disclaimer)
16. Staff-mediated dispute workflow
17. Partner visibility (authorized status updates)
18. Platform glimpse (dashboard / pipeline — Phase 4)

### 19–26 · Process & ops

19. Referral in 3–5 minutes
20. Journey map (illustrative timelines — not promises)
21. Document readiness
22. Reporting cadence (weekly / monthly / QBR)
23. Training for branch staff (RESPA-aware)
24. Compliance posture summary
25. Sample pipeline stages
26. Hand-back criteria (mortgage ready ≠ approved)

### 27–33 · Why partners choose LRP

27. More organized borrower conversations
28. Protected LO time
29. Consistent language & materials
30. Transparent progress
31. Fits credit unions / community banks / brokers
32. Co-branded enablement (approved assets only)
33. Case vignette (composite; no fabricated FICO)

### 34–40 · Close

34. Partnership package contents
35. Onboarding in 30 days
36. Commercial model placeholder — _[counsel/finance]_
37. FAQs (approval? scores? exclusivity?)
38. Next step — discovery call / pilot
39. Contact / QR
40. Closing title + disclaimer footer

## Speaker notes (key)

- Never say “we’ll get them approved.”
- Score slides must show advisory disclaimer.
- Emphasize non-exclusive + no required-use.
- Offer LO Quick Start leave-behind after meeting.

## Design notes

Follow brand style guide (Phase 3 marketing kit). Full-bleed hero optional for title only; rest clean professional slides — avoid purple-glow AI clichés.
