# Homebuyer Seminar (40 slides)

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

Educational seminar for consumers. No guaranteed approval language. Coordinate with partner LO/realtor for Q&A on lending/real estate (they own those answers).

## Slide map

### Welcome (1–5)

1. Title
2. Who’s in the room
3. Disclaimer
4. Agenda
5. What “lending ready” means

### Foundations (6–15)

6. Credit reports vs scores (education)
7. Why lenders ask for docs
8. DTI concept (illustrative)
9. Utilization basics
10. Common roadblocks
11. Collections & medical (high level)
12. Identity theft / mixed files (when to get help)
13. Myths vs facts (wipe credit, guaranteed approval)
14. Your team: realtor · LO · LRP
15. Break / Q&A micro

### Action (16–28)

16. Goal worksheet intro
17. Document checklist
18. Building a 90-day habit
19. How disputes work (staff-reviewed; no DIY hype)
20. Monitoring mindset
21. Savings / down payment habits
22. Working with your LO
23. Timeline ranges (not promises)
24. Lending Readiness Score™ explained + disclaimer
25. Sample roadmap
26. What success looks like (organization, not a magic number)
27. Red flags / scams to avoid
28. Resources

### Close (29–40)

29–35. Extended Q&A prompts  
36. How to get started with LRP  
37. Partner LO intro (if co-hosting)  
38. Realtor intro (if co-hosting)  
39. Contact / booking  
40. Thank you + disclaimer

## Facilitation

- Hand out Goal Worksheet + Document Checklist
- Collect optional interest forms (TCPA/email consents)
- No on-stage fabricated before/after FICO screenshots
