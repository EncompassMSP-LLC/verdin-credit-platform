# Leave-Behinds

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

Companion pieces for desks, counters, and partner drops. Phase 3 detail: [`14-leave-behind-materials.md`](../../../build-bible/volumes/07-marketing-launch/partner-kit/phase-3/14-leave-behind-materials.md).

---

## Desk cards (3.5" × 2" or 4" × 3")

**Front:** Wordmark · “Mortgage Readiness Partnership” · tagline  
**Back:** QR to `/resources/partner-kit` · phone · short disclaimer  
**Stock:** 16 pt matte or soft-touch  
**File:** `LRP_DeskCard_YYYYMM_v#.pdf`

---

## Business cards (standard)

| Side  | Content                                        |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Front | Wordmark · name · title · phone · email        |
| Back  | Tagline · QR to digital kit · short disclaimer |

No score badges. No “guaranteed approval” lines.

---

## Referral pads (50-sheet)

**Header:** Lending Readiness Partners — Referral Note

**Fields:**

- Borrower name
- Phone
- Product intent
- Known issues (high-level; no SSN / full report notes)
- LO / realtor name
- Date

**Footer:** Submit via secure form · `/resources/partner-kit/referral` · short disclaimer  
**Tear-off:** Branding strip with QR  
**File:** `LRP_ReferralPad_YYYYMM_v#.pdf`

---

## Counter displays (acrylic insert)

Insert size per acrylic (often 4×9 or 5×7). Prefer rack-card art.  
**Headline option:** Ask about mortgage readiness support.  
**QR:** kit hub or audience landing.

---

## Table tents (two-sided)

| Side | Message                                       |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------- |
| A    | Partner With Us (B2B) — briefing CTA          |
| B    | Didn’t Qualify? Ask your LO (borrower-facing) |

Short disclaimer both sides. Size: typical 4" × 6" folded tent.

## LO quick-reference card (laminated)

Pocket card summarizing:

1. When to refer
2. What to say / not say
3. How to submit a referral
4. Where updates appear
5. Short disclaimer

Aligns with Section 1 LO Quick Start. File: `LRP_LO_QuickRef_Card_YYYYMM_v#.pdf`
