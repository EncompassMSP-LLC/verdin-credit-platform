## A. Tri-fold (8.5" × 11" flat)

### Cover (outside front)

- Brand: Lending Readiness Partners (hero)
- Program: Mortgage Readiness Program
- Tagline
- Support: Education, strategy, and progress for the next financing opportunity.
- Visual: Full-bleed atmospheric homeownership plane (not credit-repair clichés)
- QR: `/lenders` · “Partner briefing”

### Back panel (contact)

```
Lending Readiness Partners
Partnerships
[phone] · [email]
[website]
```

QR: `/contact?intent=lender` · footer disclaimer

### Inside flap (first reveal)

**Headline:** When “not yet” needs a plan  
**Body:** Motivated borrowers stall when credit and documentation gaps are unclear. Lending Readiness Partners helps organize the path forward—while keeping loan officers and realtors informed.

### Inside panels (summary)

| Panel  | Job                                                                        |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Left   | Why partners choose LRP (visibility, plans, updates, claim-safe marketing) |
| Center | How it works: Refer → Plan → Update → Return (7-step advisory path)        |
| Right  | What partners get + CTA: Schedule a briefing                               |

**Callout (center):** We support readiness. Your team underwrites.
