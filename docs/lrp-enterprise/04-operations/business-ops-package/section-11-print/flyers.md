## Co-brand rules

- Partner logo secondary; LRP wordmark remains primary on shared drops unless a written co-brand agreement says otherwise.
- Partner may not add approval/funding claims to LRP art.
- White-label variants require ops + compliance sign-off.

## Print checklist

- [ ] CMYK + 0.125" bleed
- [ ] QR tested
- [ ] Disclaimer ≥ 7 pt
- [ ] File names `LRP_Flyer_[Name]_YYYYMM_v#.pdf`
