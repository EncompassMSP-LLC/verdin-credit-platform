# Rack Cards

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field  | Value                                                    |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Size   | 4" × 9"                                                  |
| Stock  | 14–16 pt matte                                           |
| Layout | Front: brand + one job. Back: process + QR + disclaimer. |

Phase 3 companion: [`09-rack-cards.md`](../../../build-bible/volumes/07-marketing-launch/partner-kit/phase-3/09-rack-cards.md).

---

## Lender / LO version

**Front**

- Wordmark (hero)
- Headline: Keep more borrowers in the conversation
- Support: Mortgage Readiness Partnership—advisory plans and partner visibility.
- Tagline

**Back**

- Refer → Plan → Update → Return
- Benefits micro-list (visibility · training · claim-safe language)
- QR → `/lenders`
- Short disclaimer

---

## Realtor version

**Front**

- Wordmark
- Headline: When financing needs more time
- Support: Keep buyers engaged with a dignity-first readiness path alongside your preferred lender.
- Tagline

**Back**

- What agents can say / not say (3 bullets; no approval promises)
- QR → `/realtors`
- Short disclaimer

---

## Borrower version

**Front**

- Wordmark
- Headline: A clear plan for “not yet”
- Support: Education and next steps toward your next financing conversation.
- Tagline

**Back**

- What to expect
- Portal mention (invite via LO)
- QR → ask LO / `/contact?intent=borrower`
- Short disclaimer · no approval promises

## Attorney / advisor version (optional fourth)

**Front**

- Wordmark
- Headline: Readiness support for clients with financing goals
- Support: Advisory education and documentation habits—separate from legal advice and underwriting.

**Back**

- Referral path overview
- QR → `/contact?intent=partner`
