## 1. Purpose — Referral Relationship

1.1 Partner may refer consumers who may benefit from education, strategy, and progress tracking related to mortgage readiness (“Referred Clients”) to LRP.

1.2 LRP provides **advisory credit-readiness and documentation support**. LRP does **not**:

- originate, underwrite, or fund loans;
- guarantee loan approval, pre-approval, or terms;
- act as Partner’s agent for lending decisions;
- file bureau disputes without staff review and applicable consumer authorization.

1.3 This Agreement establishes a **referral relationship** only. It does not create a joint venture, partnership for tax purposes, employment, or franchise.

---

## 2. Non-Exclusive Partnership

2.1 The relationship is **non-exclusive**. Partner may refer clients to other service providers. LRP may accept referrals from other partners and market its services independently.

2.2 Neither party is obligated to refer a minimum volume of clients.

---

## 3. Referral Process

3.1 Partner submits referrals using LRP’s approved referral form, partner portal, or other written method designated by LRP.

3.2 Required minimum referral data (as applicable and permitted): borrower name, contact method, referring loan officer, company, and high-level mortgage goal (e.g., purchase vs refinance). Partner shall not send Social Security numbers or full credit reports via unsecured email.

3.3 LRP will acknowledge referral receipt within **[two (2) business days]** and schedule an initial consultation subject to client responsiveness.

3.4 Client engagement with LRP is voluntary. Clients may decline services at any time.

---

## 4. Fees and Compensation

4.1 **[Counsel to complete.]** Describe whether services are paid by the consumer, Partner, or a permitted arrangement. Any compensation must comply with RESPA, state law, and applicable mortgage regulations.

4.2 Unless expressly agreed in a separate signed schedule, **no referral fees, kickbacks, or thing-of-value exchanges** are authorized under this Agreement.

4.3 Partner shall not condition loan pricing, approval recommendations, or credit decisions on a client’s use of LRP.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall protect the other’s non-public business information and any consumer information received in connection with referrals (“Confidential Information”).

5.2 Confidential Information excludes information that is public (other than by breach), independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law (with notice where legally permitted).

5.3 Consumer information shall be handled under LRP’s privacy and information-security policies and applicable law (including FCRA where LRP obtains or uses consumer reports).

5.4 Obligations survive termination for **[three (3) years]**, or longer for consumer data as required by law or retention policy.

---

## 6. Communication Expectations

6.1 LRP will provide Partner with **status updates** on Referred Clients who have authorized partner visibility, using agreed channels (portal, email, scheduled reports). Updates describe **progress and readiness work**, not underwriting decisions.

6.2 Partner shall not represent LRP’s Lending Readiness Score™ (or similar advisory indicators) as a FICO® score, CRA credit score, or loan approval.

6.3 Both parties shall use claim-library-safe language: education, strategy, and preparation for a lender conversation — **not** guaranteed approval or “we’ll get you funded.”

6.4 Escalations: Partner primary contact ↔ LRP partner success contact within **[one (1) business day]** for urgent client issues.

---

## 7. Compliance Statement

7.1 Each party represents it will comply with applicable federal and state laws, including (as applicable) RESPA, TILA, FCRA, GLBA, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, state mortgage licensing, and unfair/deceptive practices laws.

7.2 Partner is solely responsible for its own licensing, advertising, and loan origination compliance.

7.3 LRP is solely responsible for its consumer-facing service disclosures, dispute documentation standards, and staff-mediated workflows.

7.4 Marketing materials that jointly mention both brands require mutual written approval (email sufficient) before public use.

---

## 8. Intellectual Property and Branding

8.1 Each party retains ownership of its trademarks, materials, and software.

8.2 Limited license: during the term, Partner may use LRP’s name and approved logos solely to describe the referral relationship, consistent with LRP brand guidelines. LRP may list Partner as a referring partner unless Partner opts out in writing.

---

## 9. Term and Termination

9.1 Term begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.

9.2 Either party may terminate **for convenience** upon **[thirty (30) days]** written notice.

9.3 Either party may terminate **immediately** for material breach not cured within **[ten (10) days]** after notice, or for unlawful conduct, loss of required license, or insolvency events.

9.4 Upon termination: (a) new referrals cease; (b) LRP may continue serving already-engaged clients under LRP’s client agreements; (c) Partner portal access may be revoked; (d) each party returns or destroys the other’s Confidential Information upon request, subject to legal retention.

---

## 10. Limitation of Liability; Disclaimer

10.1 LRP’s services are advisory. Outcomes depend on consumer circumstances, creditor/bureau responses, underwriting guidelines, and market conditions. **No guarantee of score improvement, account deletion, approval, or funding.**

10.2 TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. Aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to **[counsel to set]**.

10.3 Nothing in this section limits liability for willful misconduct, fraud, or amounts that cannot be limited by law.
