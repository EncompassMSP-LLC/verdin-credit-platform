# Office Training Manual

**Lending Readiness Partners — Mortgage Partner Program**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field         | Value                                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Audience      | Branch managers, processors, LOAs, reception                        |
| Goal          | Consistent referrals, clean expectations, RESPA-aware communication |
| Prerequisites | Partner Welcome Guide; claim library awareness                      |

---

## 1. Training objectives

After this training, staff can:

1. Use referral forms / portal correctly
2. Explain the program in claim-safe language
3. Set realistic borrower expectations
4. Avoid common RESPA and advertising pitfalls
5. Follow the communication workflow with LRP

**Suggested delivery:** 45–60 minutes + role-play. Refresh quarterly.

---

## 2. Using referral forms

### 2.1 Approved channels

| Channel                 | Use when                   |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Partner portal          | Default                    |
| Secure PDF / paper form | Offline LO / trade show    |
| Ops-assisted intake     | Escalation / accessibility |

### 2.2 Required fields

- Loan officer + company
- Borrower legal name
- Preferred phone and/or email
- Mortgage goal (purchase / refi / other)
- Consent that borrower expects contact

### 2.3 Do not put on the form or in email

- Full SSN
- Full account numbers
- Unencrypted credit report PDFs to personal email
- Medical details unrelated to the referral

### 2.4 Quality bar

A “good” referral includes a short note: _“High utilization + medical collection; targeting purchase in ~6 months.”_

A “poor” referral: name only, no contact, borrower unaware.

### 2.5 Practice drill

Have each trainee submit a **test** referral in sandbox/demo, then find it on the pipeline view.

## 3. Explaining the program

### 3.1 Approved short explanation

> Lending Readiness Partners helps borrowers get organized for a mortgage conversation — education, strategy, and progress tracking. They are not the lender and do not approve loans.

### 3.2 Role cards

| Role            | One-liner                                                              |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reception       | “We can connect you with our readiness partner; your LO will explain.” |
| LOA / processor | “LRP handles the readiness plan; we handle the loan file.”             |
| LO              | Use Quick Start pitch; capture referral                                |

### 3.3 Visual aid

Keep the LO Quick Start and a one-page leave-behind at the desk. Do not invent new claims on social or whiteboard.

## 4. Setting expectations

### 4.1 Always say

- Advisory / preparation
- Timelines vary
- Borrower must engage and send documents
- Partner updates require authorization

### 4.2 Never say

- “They’ll get you approved.”
- “We’ll raise your score 100 points.”
- “They wipe negatives automatically.”
- “AI files everything for you.”
- “You have to use them to get our best rate.” (prohibited conditioning)

### 4.3 Handling disappointment

If a borrower expected overnight miracles:

1. Reset to education + plan
2. Offer to loop in the LO
3. Document the conversation
4. Do not over-promise to retain the referral

## 5. Avoiding RESPA concerns

> Training summary only — not legal advice. Escalate edge cases to compliance counsel.

### 5.1 Core ideas for staff

1. **No kickbacks** — Do not accept/offer cash, gifts, or “spiffs” for referrals unless a counsel-approved compliant arrangement exists in writing.
2. **No required use** — Borrowers choose whether to engage LRP. Loan terms must not be conditioned on using LRP.
3. **Truthful marketing** — Co-branded pieces need approval.
4. **Separate relationships** — LRP’s consumer agreement ≠ your loan disclosures.
5. **Fair lending** — Refer based on borrower need/interest, not protected-class stereotypes.

### 5.2 Red-flag phrases (stop and rewrite)

| Red flag                            | Safer rewrite                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| “We’ll pay you per deal you send”   | Stop — escalate to compliance                                                              |
| “Use LRP or we can’t proceed”       | “LRP is optional support if you want a readiness plan.”                                    |
| “Guaranteed pre-approval after LRP” | “LRP prepares you for a lender conversation; approval is always our/underwriter decision.” |

### 5.3 Gifts & entertainment

Follow your company’s RESPA gift policy. When in doubt, decline and document.

## 6. Communication workflow

### 6.1 Happy path

```text
Borrower interest
    → LO expectation + referral
    → LRP acknowledgment
    → Consultation / intake
    → Status updates (authorized)
    → Mortgage-ready hand-back
    → Partner loan process
```

### 6.2 Who speaks when

| Topic                                  | Primary speaker                                      |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Loan pricing, programs, underwriting   | Partner / LO                                         |
| Readiness plan, disputes, doc coaching | LRP                                                  |
| Conflicting advice                     | Joint call; document resolution                      |
| Complaints about LRP                   | LRP CS + notify Partner Success                      |
| Complaints about loan                  | Partner compliance; notify LRP if readiness impacted |

### 6.3 SLA reminders

| Event                       | Target                             |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| LRP ack of referral         | 2 business days                    |
| Partner escalation response | 1 business day                     |
| Stuck file with no update   | LO pings Ops after 7 business days |

### 6.4 Channel hygiene

- Prefer portal notes for PHI/credit-adjacent detail
- Use email for scheduling and non-sensitive status
- SMS only with proper consent (TCPA)
- Never CC large distribution lists on borrower credit details

### 6.5 Lost opportunity

If borrower withdraws or ghosts:

1. LRP marks appropriate stage (e.g., Lost Opportunity / Withdrawn)
2. LO notified
3. Door left open for re-referral later — no pressure tactics

## 7. Role-play scenarios

### Scenario A — Skeptical borrower

Borrower: “Is this just credit repair that doesn’t work?”  
Trainee should: explain advisory readiness, staff review, no guarantees, optional engagement.

### Scenario B — LO overpromises

LO: “Sign up and you’ll be approved in 30 days.”  
Manager coaching: correct live; restate claim-safe language; document coaching.

### Scenario C — Processor asked for SSN by email

Correct action: refuse insecure channel; use portal/secure upload; cite privacy training.
