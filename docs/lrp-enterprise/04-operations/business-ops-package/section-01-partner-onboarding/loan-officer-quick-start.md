## Page 1 — Submit a referral

### One-sentence pitch to the borrower

> “Lending Readiness Partners helps you organize credit and documents so you’re better prepared for a mortgage conversation. They’re not the lender and can’t guarantee approval — but they’ll give you a clear plan and keep me updated if you say that’s okay.”

### Steps

1. Confirm the borrower expects LRP outreach.
2. Open the partner portal **or** complete the paper/PDF referral form.
3. Enter: your name/NMLS, company, borrower name, best phone/email, mortgage goal, target timing, top credit concerns.
4. Do **not** email Social Security numbers or full credit PDFs to personal inboxes.
5. Submit. Save the **Referral Number**.

### Time box

Most referrals take **3–5 minutes** once you have contact info.

## Page 2 — Required documents

Borrowers gather these with LRP (you can start the list):

| Priority       | Item                                                 |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Required       | Government-issued photo ID                           |
| As applicable  | Social Security card / proof of SSN                  |
| Income         | Pay stubs, W-2s, tax returns                         |
| Assets         | Bank statements                                      |
| Credit context | Credit reports they already have; collection letters |
| Mortgage       | Existing pre-approval (context only)                 |
| Other          | Supporting documentation for disputes/errors         |

LRP’s full Document Checklist lives in the Client Intake Package.

## Page 3 — What happens next

| Step                   | Owner          | What you see                                          |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Acknowledgment         | LRP            | Email/portal note within 2 business days              |
| Consultation scheduled | LRP + borrower | Stage moves (e.g., Consultation Scheduled / Intake)   |
| Documents received     | Borrower + LRP | Milestone update                                      |
| Audit / plan           | LRP            | Initial Assessment / readiness report (if authorized) |
| Active readiness work  | LRP            | Dispute / debt-reduction / monitoring stages          |
| Mortgage ready         | LRP → you      | Hand-back; you resume loan process                    |
| Closed / lost          | Partner / LRP  | Final status                                          |

**Remember:** Pipeline stages are **workflow**, not underwriting.

## Page 4 — Client timeline & status updates

### How to talk about time

Use ranges, not promises:

- “Many borrowers work a plan over weeks to months — your situation sets the pace.”
- “Creditors and bureaus control their own response times.”

### Status updates

- Watch portal notifications or weekly digest.
- Ask Partner Success if a file looks stuck > **[7]** business days with no note.
- Never invent score changes for the borrower.

### Lending Readiness Score™

Advisory only. Not FICO. Not approval.

## Page 5 — Common questions

**Can I promise they’ll be approved after LRP?**  
No.

**Can I charge them a referral fee to LRP?**  
Only if counsel has approved a compliant structure. Default: no referral fees.

**What if they ask “will my score go up 100 points?”**  
“LRP doesn’t guarantee score changes. They focus on a plan and progress.”

**What if I’m competing with another LO at my shop?**  
Referrals are attributed to the submitting LO. Follow your company’s rules.

**Who do I call?**  
Partner Success / Ops Desk — see Welcome Guide contact matrix.

**Where’s the longer playbook?**  
Partner Welcome Guide + Office Training Manual.
