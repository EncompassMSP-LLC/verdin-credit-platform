# Part A — Welcome

## 1. Welcome to Lending Readiness Partners

Thank you for partnering with **Lending Readiness Partners (LRP)**.

We exist so more borrowers can prepare for a productive lender conversation — with clearer documentation habits, organized credit work, and transparent progress your team can see (when the client authorizes it).

We are **not** a lender, creditor, or underwriter. We do **not** guarantee approval, pre-approval, or funding. We help borrowers get **lending ready** through education, strategy, and progress tracking.

**Tagline:** Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready.

## 2. What “lending ready” means here

“Lending ready” means the borrower has made meaningful progress on:

- Understanding their credit and documentation picture
- Completing agreed readiness tasks
- Organizing supporting documents
- Preparing for questions a lender may ask

It does **not** mean they are approved, pre-approved, or “cleared to close.”

Our **Lending Readiness Score™** is an **advisory** indicator for organizing work. It is **not** a FICO® score, not a consumer reporting agency score, and not an underwriting decision.

## 3. Mission

Help mortgage professionals serve more borrowers by pairing origination expertise with a disciplined readiness partner — so fewer files stall for preventable credit and documentation gaps.

# Part C — How the partnership works

## 5. Roles

| Role              | Owns                                                                                           |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Partner (you)** | Origination relationship, loan process, underwriting with your investors/investors’ guidelines |
| **Loan officer**  | Submits referrals, sets borrower expectations, receives status updates                         |
| **LRP**           | Intake, consultation, readiness plan, staff-mediated credit work, progress reporting           |
| **Borrower**      | Engagement, authorizations, documents, decisions                                               |

## 6. Non-exclusive by design

You may refer elsewhere. We may work with other lenders. Volume minimums are not required. Quality of handoff and clear expectations matter more than raw count.

## 7. What LRP does / does not do

### We do

- Structured credit consultation and roadmap
- Document checklists aligned to mortgage readiness
- Staff-mediated dispute preparation and tracking (where appropriate)
- Debt-reduction and utilization coaching (educational)
- Progress updates and readiness reports for authorized partners
- Hand-back when the borrower is mortgage-ready **for your process**

### We do not

- Originate or underwrite loans
- Promise score outcomes or approval
- Accept RESPA-prohibited referral fees (unless counsel-approved separate structure)
- File unsupervised or automated bureau actions without staff review
- Make legal conclusions about FCRA “violations”

# Part D — Referral process

## 8. When to refer

Good referral signals:

- Borrower wants to buy/refinance but credit or documentation is not ready
- Thin file / past derogs / high utilization / collections clutter
- Borrower needs a structured plan and accountability
- You want visibility into progress without becoming the credit coach

Defer referral (or set different expectations) when:

- Borrower needs only a same-week pre-approval with clean credit
- Borrower refuses any engagement with a readiness partner
- Immediate hardship legal advice is needed (refer to counsel / housing counselor as appropriate)

## 9. How to submit a referral

1. Confirm the borrower expects a call/email from LRP.
2. Complete the referral form / partner portal fields:
   - Referral number (system-assigned) or your internal ID
   - Loan officer name / NMLS
   - Company
   - Borrower name + preferred contact
   - Mortgage goal + target timeline (if known)
   - High-level credit concerns (no SSN via email)
3. Attach only what is permitted and secure (or note “docs with LRP at intake”).
4. LRP acknowledges within **two business days**.

## 10. Required / helpful documents (borrower-side)

See Client Intake Package for full checklist. Common items:

- Government ID
- Recent pay stubs / W-2 / tax returns (as relevant)
- Bank statements
- Credit reports the borrower already has (optional)
- Collection letters / supporting docs
- Any pre-approval letter already issued (for context — not a promise we will match it)

## 11. Consent and privacy

Borrowers authorize LRP services and, separately, whether Partner may receive status updates. Without authorization, LRP shares only aggregate or no client-level detail.

# Part G — Client expectations

## 16. What we ask of referred clients

- Honest information and timely documents
- Reading disclosures and providing required authorizations
- Completing assigned readiness tasks
- Communicating life changes that affect income/housing goals
- Understanding that LRP advice is educational/advisory

## 17. What clients should expect from LRP

- Professional intake and clear roadmap
- Respectful communication
- Progress tracking
- Staff review before dispute sending

# Part H — Compliance overview

## 18. RESPA-aware partnership

- No unapproved referral fees or “things of value” for referrals
- Do not require use of LRP as a condition of loan terms
- Keep marketing accurate and jointly approved when co-branded
- Separate LRP consumer agreements from your loan disclosures

## 19. FCRA / privacy (high level)

When LRP obtains or uses consumer report information, FCRA and privacy rules apply. Partners should not forward full credit files through insecure channels. See Compliance Package (Section 3).

## 20. Advertising rules of thumb

- Use approved one-pagers and decks
- No guaranteed approval language
- No fabricated testimonials or score screenshots
- Disclose relationship when required

# Part I — FAQs

**Q: Will working with LRP guarantee my borrower gets approved?**  
A: No. Approval is always the lender’s decision.

**Q: Is the Lending Readiness Score™ a credit score?**  
A: No. It is an advisory preparation indicator.

**Q: Can LOs promise a score increase?**  
A: No. Outcomes vary; we track tasks and progress, not guaranteed FICO changes.

**Q: Who talks to the borrower day to day?**  
A: LRP for readiness work; you for the loan. Coordinate — don’t contradict.

**Q: What if the borrower goes silent?**  
A: We attempt outreach per SOP, update status (e.g., lost opportunity), and notify you.

**Q: Can we white-label?**  
A: Platform white-label options are part of the Lending Readiness Platform™ roadmap; marketing co-brand uses approved assets.

**Q: How do we terminate?**  
A: Per the Partner Agreement — typically notice for convenience; clients already engaged may continue with LRP.

**Q: Where do binaries / print files live?**  
A: Partner kit assets under `assets/lrp/` and the marketing package; ops manuscripts under this Business Operations Package.
