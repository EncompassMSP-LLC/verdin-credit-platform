# Complaint Resolution Process

**Lending Readiness Partners**  
_Helping More Borrowers Become Lending Ready._

| Field        | Value                                  |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Status       | **DRAFT — compliance review required** |
| Intake email | compliance@[domain] / cs@[domain]      |

---

## 1. What counts as a complaint

Any expression of dissatisfaction about LRP services, communications, billing, privacy, partner handoffs, or staff conduct — verbal or written.

## 2. Intake

| Step | Action                                                   | SLA                         |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 1    | Log complaint (date, channel, client, summary, severity) | Same day                    |
| 2    | Acknowledge to complainant                               | **1 business day**          |
| 3    | Assign owner (CS / Compliance)                           | Same day as ack             |
| 4    | Investigate (files, call notes, partner context)         | Target **10 business days** |
| 5    | Response with outcome / next steps                       | Within investigation window |
| 6    | Close & retain per retention policy                      | After resolution            |

## 3. Severity

| Level  | Examples                                                | Extra action              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Low    | Timing / scheduling friction                            | CS owner                  |
| Medium | Billing dispute, unclear advice                         | Manager + Compliance CC   |
| High   | Privacy breach allegation, discrimination, legal threat | Compliance lead + counsel |

## 4. Partner-related complaints

If complaint involves a referring LO/lender relationship: notify Partner Success; do not disclose unrelated client data to the partner.

## 5. No retaliation

Staff must not retaliate against clients who complain in good faith.

## 6. Metrics (ops)

Track volume, time-to-ack, time-to-close, reopen rate — for monthly compliance review.
