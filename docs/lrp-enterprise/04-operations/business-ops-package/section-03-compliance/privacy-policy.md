## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Lending Readiness Partners (“LRP,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information in connection with our readiness services, partner referrals, websites, and portals.

## 2. Information we collect

| Category                   | Examples                                                                                                |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity & contact         | Name, address, email, phone, date of birth                                                              |
| Referral metadata          | Referring LO, company, referral number, goals                                                           |
| Financial / credit-related | Income estimates, debts, documents you upload, consumer report information we obtain with authorization |
| Technical                  | IP address, device/browser data, portal usage logs                                                      |
| Communications             | Emails, calls notes, SMS (with consent)                                                                 |

We do not require you to provide more sensitive data than needed for the service.

## 3. How we use information

- Provide consultations, roadmaps, and progress tracking
- Prepare and track staff-mediated dispute-related work where appropriate
- Operate partner status updates **when you authorize sharing**
- Secure our systems, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations
- Improve services (aggregated / de-identified where feasible)

We do **not** use your information to originate or underwrite a mortgage.

## 4. How we share information

| Recipient                               | When                                                     |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Service providers                       | Hosting, email, SMS, document storage — under contract   |
| Referring mortgage partner              | Only with your authorization for status/progress         |
| Consumer reporting agencies / creditors | As needed for authorized dispute or information requests |
| Legal / regulators                      | When required by law or to protect rights                |

We do not sell personal information for money. **[Counsel: CCPA/state “sale/share” analysis.]**

## 5. Your choices

- Access / correction requests: privacy@[domain]
- Withdraw partner-sharing authorization (may limit LO visibility)
- Marketing email opt-out
- SMS stop instructions (where TCPA applies)
