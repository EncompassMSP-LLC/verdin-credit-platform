## Disclosure

Lending Readiness Partners (“LRP,” “we”) may obtain and/or use consumer report information about you from one or more consumer reporting agencies, and may review credit-related information you provide, for the purpose of providing **advisory credit-readiness and documentation support**.

LRP is **not** a creditor, lender, or underwriter. LRP does **not** make credit decisions, guarantee loan approval, or guarantee any particular credit score outcome.

If we take adverse action based in whole or in part on information in a consumer report (where applicable), we will provide any notices required by the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) and related laws.

You have rights under the FCRA, including the right to obtain information about your file from consumer reporting agencies and to dispute inaccurate or incomplete information with those agencies. For a summary of rights, see the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau’s “A Summary of Your Rights Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act.”
